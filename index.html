Hexhaven

v1.8 — installable PWA, launches full-screen with no browser bars

Settle the island, trade what you have for what you need, and be first to ten points. Play three bots on your own, or pass one device around the table.

How a turn goes. Roll the dice — every hex with that number pays its neighbours. Then trade and build in any order. A 7 sends the robber: anyone holding more than 7 cards loses half.

Costs. Road: lumber + brick. Settlement: lumber + brick + grain + wool. City: 3 ore + 2 grain. Development card: ore + grain + wool.
